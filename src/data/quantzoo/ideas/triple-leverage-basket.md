## the idea

i think holding a few funds that move 3x the market will beat just holding the s&p over the long run, as long as i get out when the market turns ugly.

## why

the tech and s&p indexes have gone up most years for a long time, and these funds give you 3x every day's move. the catch is the crashes: a fund that falls 67% has to triple just to get back. so the whole game is not being fully in during the bad years.

## how i'd play it

buy three funds in roughly equal amounts: one that goes 3x the big tech index (TQQQ), one that goes 3x the s&p 500 (UPRO), and one that goes 3x chip companies (SOXL). when the s&p closes below its average price of the last 200 days, sell and sit in cash. when the funds are swinging around a lot, hold less of them.

## what would prove me wrong

a long sideways market where these funds slowly bleed, or a crash so fast that the 200 day rule gets me out after the damage is done. if it trails just holding the s&p over a few years, the idea is wrong.
